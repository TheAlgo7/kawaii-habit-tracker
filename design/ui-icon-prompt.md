# Generated UI artwork brief

These assets were generated with the built-in Imagegen tool and translated into optimized transparent WebP files under `public/ui-icons/`.

## Theme and walking atlas

Create a two-by-two atlas on a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key background. Match the approved watercolor cat illustration: hand-painted storybook gouache, warm brown ink outlines, restrained internal paper texture, slightly irregular contours, strong silhouettes, and an all-ages tone. The four subjects are a terracotta sun with olive leaves, an aubergine crescent with a lantern-gold star, a sage matcha cup on a closed book with a sprout, and walking shoes with a small sprout and motion line. Keep one centered subject per quadrant, generous padding, no text, no faces, no emoji styling, no dividers, and no shadows.

## Habit atlas

Create a matching two-by-two atlas on the same flat chroma-key background. The four subjects are a rolled sage movement mat with terracotta ribbon, a meditation cushion with balanced stones and a quiet breath curl, a woven tidy basket with folded cloth and olive sprig, and a bowl containing an apple, pear, and leafy greens. Preserve the same line weight, watercolor texture, visual weight, and color restraint as the first atlas. No text, people, faces, emoji styling, frames, or shadows.

The chroma background is removed with the Imagegen skill helper using a soft matte and despill. Each final asset is resized to 192 by 192 pixels and encoded as transparent WebP.
