# Competitor Research and Product Decisions

Reviewed: 2 August 2026

This note records the market patterns used for the version 1.1 redesign. It is not a feature checklist. The purpose was to identify what makes a daily habit app fast and trustworthy, then combine that utility with an original companion and world instead of copying another product’s surface style.

## Products reviewed

| Product | Strong pattern | Decision carried into Kawaii Habits | Pattern deliberately not copied |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Finch](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/42149821015693-New-User-Guide) | A companion gives ordinary self-care emotional meaning and onboarding creates attachment early. | Name the companion and garden, complete one real tiny habit during onboarding, and let care visibly affect Neko’s world. | Broad wellness scope, dense reward economy, and a long catalogue of unrelated exercises. |
| [HabitKit](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.roehl.habitkit&hl=en_US) | A visual grid makes recent consistency understandable at a glance. | Seven dated marks on each habit plus a dedicated monthly Rhythm trail. | Turning the main Today screen into a wall of colored tiles. |
| [Streaks](https://streaks.app/) | Very fast completion, strong icon customization, and focused daily utility. | One large completion control, first-party icon choices, custom schedules, and an uncluttered agenda. | A brittle number that makes one missed day feel like total loss. |
| [everyday](https://everyday.app/features) | A calendar chain makes repetition and gaps legible over time. | Schedule-aware monthly history, visible rest, tiny, partial, and cared states. | Treating every unscheduled or resting day as an empty failure cell. |

The earlier prototype audit also examined Productive, Habitify, Habitica, and WaterDo. Those products reinforced two boundaries: guided plans and game loops can help discovery, but multiple currencies, countdown pressure, and generic task gamification would weaken this product’s quieter promise.

## Resulting product position

Kawaii Habit Tracker now combines two layers:

1. **Focused utility:** Today is scannable, completion takes one tap, schedules and local dates are reliable, history is readable, and Undo is adjacent to the action.
2. **Original attachment:** the approved watercolor cat, garden scenes, care points, keepsakes, and recovery-first voice make returning emotionally worthwhile.

## Implemented differentiators

- Tiny versions are a first-class completion state, not a hidden note.
- Planned rest is visible and excluded from the completion denominator.
- Archived habits remain in historical Rhythm calculations.
- The garden grows from lifetime care while the main screen stays free of currencies and game dashboards.
- Neko’s mood can become quiet after a real gap, but never punishes a new user or blocks progress.
- Sunlit Garden and Moonlit Nook are the same component system under different light, not separate visual products.
- Data remains account-free, exportable, and local to the device.

## Launch boundaries

- No social ranking, streak loss, ads, subscription wall, or public profile.
- No unreliable background-reminder claim for an installable browser app.
- No platform emoji used as product artwork.
- No generic AI chat outside routines, reflection, recovery, and app guidance.
